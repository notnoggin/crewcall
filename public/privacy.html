Crewcall Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what information Crewcall collects and how it is used. Crewcall is operated by Olamide Ilori, based in Nigeria. Contact: hello.olamideilori@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

  • From business owners and team members (via Whop): Whop user ID, name, username, and business/company ID, so we can show your dashboard and settings.
  • From applicants: the details they enter in an application (for example name, Whop username, contact details, portfolio or social links, rates, availability and answers to role questions).
  • Roster data you import: names, handles, status, capacity, rates and dates you upload by CSV.
  • Messages: status messages you send to applicants through Whop DMs or support chats from inside Crewcall.
  • Payment status: confirmation from Whop that a plan is active. Payments are processed by Whop; we never receive card details.

2. How we use it

Only to run Crewcall: showing applications to the business they were sent to, shortlisting and roster management, notifying owners of new applications, sending status updates to applicants, and checking plan access. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Sharing

Application data is visible to the business it was submitted to and its authorized team members. We use service providers to host the app and database (such as Vercel and Supabase) and Whop for accounts, messaging and payments. We may disclose data if required by law.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored with our hosting/database providers and protected with access controls. No method is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep data while the business uses Crewcall. Business owners can delete applications and roster entries, and anyone can ask us to delete or export their data by emailing hello.olamideilori@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data. Email us to use these rights.

7. Children

Crewcall is not intended for anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
